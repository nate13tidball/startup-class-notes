## Slide 16

[View slide in original PDF](../../../presentations/Raison_detre_-_Josh_Santos.pdf#page=16)

Embedded text:

````text
Exercise 1: Reverse Eulogy
11 min · pair up · output: what you stood for

  Somebody you respect is giving a toast at your funeral.
   They never mention your company or work by name. 

   What do they say you stood for?

   Break into pairs and share. Listeners share their favorite
   line with the speaker
````

Exercise 2: Digging Beyond the Surface
12 min · same pair · output: your deepest answers


     Stay with your previous partner

      In response to the things you wrote in exercise 1,

      you?” five rounds deep

      Partner paraphrases the deepest answer back

      Swap

## Slide 18

[View slide in original PDF](../../../presentations/Raison_detre_-_Josh_Santos.pdf#page=18)

Embedded text:

````text
Exercise 3: The No-Startup Test
3 min · solo · output: list of crazy actions


  Think of something you think is broken in the world.

   What kinds of crazy things would you do to fix it there were
   no constraints, downsides, or risks of failure?

   Write a few of the craziest actions or concepts down.
````
