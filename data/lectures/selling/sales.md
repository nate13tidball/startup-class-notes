Embedded text:

````text
MEETINGS   Sales Meetings
            After the pitch, shut up. Then:


            Does this feel like it could work for your situation?


            [If yes:] What would the logical next step be on your end?


            [If they don't know:] Would it make sense to [specific next

            step]? I can have that ready by [date].







            Never leave without a next step and a date.
````
